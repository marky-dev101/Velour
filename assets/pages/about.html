WELCOME TO VELOUR

We carefully prepare every cup using quality ingredients and serve it alongside cakes and pastries made to pair perfectly with your favorite drink.

Whether you're catching up with friends, working on a project, enjoying some quiet time, or simply looking for something delicious, Velour Café is here for you.
